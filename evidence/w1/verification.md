## Privacy argument delta (for reviewers)

Only the numeric charge of hop `G0U → G1U`'s window term changes. `crtCurveWindowMass` now proves
`mass ≤ ofReal (15 ((p−1)^2+1)/M)` directly (`numer : 5 · 3 ((p−1)^2+1) ≤ 15 ((p−1)^2+1)`), instead of weakening it to
`smudgeError`. Every consumer (`Final`, `Statements`, `Instance`, `EntryInstance`, `StageTwoTable`) takes
`hiddenWindowError` abstractly, and the build re-checks all of them. The smudge term keeps its own proof (`Flags/Wrap`),
and its share increases from 7/10 to 77/50 because `M` shrinks by `41/17`.
